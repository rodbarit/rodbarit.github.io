About

I'm a quantitative researcher with more than a decade across investment management, portfolio management and systematic trading research.

Today I'm a Quantitative Researcher at Phitopolis and a Lecturer at Ateneo de Manila University. I design and refine systematic strategies, using Python, machine learning and statistical modeling to build predictive trading signals and improve portfolio performance.

Before that I managed equity portfolios at Sun Life and the Bank of the Philippine Islands, across retirement and pre-need accounts. As a UK Government Chevening Scholar I completed an MSc in Finance Analytics at King's College London, with research on empirical asset pricing via machine learning in emerging markets.

Outside research, I'm a Toastmasters charter president, a civic technology advocate and a community builder, connecting people across finance, technology and public service.

What I care about most: financial inclusion through technology — using quantitative finance, machine learning and fintech to make financial systems more accessible and equitable, particularly for underserved communities in emerging markets.
